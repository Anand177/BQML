Learning Rate
Learning rate controls the size of the step taken toward the minimum of a loss function during each iteration of training

During training, a model calculates the gradient (slope) of the loss function to know which direction to adjust its internal weights. The learning rate scales that gradient:
\(\text{New\ Weight}=\text{Old\ Weight}-(\text{Learning\ Rate}\times \text{Gradient})\)
• Too High: The steps are too large. The model can overshoot the optimal minimum, bounce around, or diverge entirely.
• Too Low: The steps are tiny. Training takes a very long time, consumes excessive resources, or risks getting stuck in a suboptimal local minimum.
• Just Right: Balances convergence speed and precision, allowing the model to smoothly settle into a low-loss solution.
